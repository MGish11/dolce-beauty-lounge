About the studio

We keep one room, a short list of services, and a long appointment — so that skin, hair, and hands are tended at the speed of craft rather than the speed of the calendar.

01 — Services

What we do,
and only that.

Six disciplines, each with a dedicated specialist. No prices here on purpose — every service is quoted after a consult, because hair and skin do not price by the hour.

  • Facials & Skin Therapy

    Diagnostic, unhurried treatment built around the skin you have today.

  • Lashes & Brows

    Extensions, lifts, and shaping calibrated to the architecture of your face.

  • Hair Styling & Color

    Dimensional color and cuts that keep their shape as they grow out.

  • Makeup Artistry

    Application for the camera, the evening, or an ordinary Tuesday.

  • Waxing

    Precise hair removal with aftercare that genuinely settles the skin.

  • Bridal & Events

    Trials, timelines, and on-site teams for mornings that cannot run late.

02 — The Space

Arched plaster, terrazzo underfoot, and light that turns gold by four.

03 — Ritual

Every appointment runs the same three movements.

  1. 01

    Consult

    We start seated, not reclined. Ten minutes on what you want, what you have already tried, and what your skin and hair are actually doing right now.

  2. 02

    Treat

    The work itself, at the pace it deserves. No upsell halfway through, no clock on the wall, no second client waiting in your chair.

  3. 03

    Restore

    Tea, a mirror in honest light, and a written plan for the weeks between visits — including what to stop doing.
